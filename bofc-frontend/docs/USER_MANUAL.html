BOFC User Manual

How to sign in, follow your tournaments, take part in player auctions, and — if you're a coordinator or admin — run the club's matches and records.

01 Getting started

Signing in

  1. Enter your email address and password.
  2. Optionally check Enable Auto Login to stay signed in on this device. Leave it unchecked on a shared or public computer.
  3. Click Sign In to Dashboard.

First time signing in

If your account was just created — by an admin, or through auction registration — you'll be asked to set a new password before continuing. Enter the temporary password you were given as "Old Password," then choose a new one (8+ characters, with an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, and a number).

Forgot your password? There's no self-service reset yet — ask a coordinator or admin to reset it from the Players list. You'll set a new password on your next login, the same way as a first-time sign-in.

Your role

What you can see and do depends on your role: Player Coordinator Admin Super Admin — each one builds on the last. If a button or menu isn't visible to you, it's usually gated to a role above yours. See the full breakdown in Roles & permissions.

02 The dashboard

Your home screen after signing in. It shows total collections, total expenses, and the current account balance; the next upcoming match; and a chart of player payments by month.

03 Your profile

Click your name or avatar anywhere in the app to open a profile page: photo, contact details, and playing position, plus stat tiles for Matches, Goals, Assists, Goals+Assists, and cards.

  • Match History — your record of wins, draws, and losses, with your contribution in each match.
  • Goalkeeping History — a timeline of rounds you played in goal.

On your own profile you'll also find tabs to edit your details and change your password. Admins can do the same for any player from the Players list.

04 Tournaments

Browsing

Open Tournaments in the sidebar. Cards are filterable by status — Upcoming, Ongoing, Concluded — and searchable by name. Each card shows the date, venue, status, and a menu of actions available to you.

Joining (RSVP)

Choose Join on a tournament to open its RSVP page. There you can see how many players are In, Out, or still Pending, and respond yourself:

  • Yes, I'm in or Can't make it, with an optional comment
  • Your comment auto-saves — no separate save button
  • Once teams are formed, a View Team button shows your side

Following a tournament — the Match Center

A tournament's own tabs (or Match Center in the sidebar) cover everything about it:

  • Fixtures — the schedule, with live scores and timers for matches in progress
  • Roadmap — a visual bracket of rounds and groups
  • Results and Table — completed matches and standings
  • Stats — top scorers and other leaderboards
  • Players — team rosters, with captain/vice-captain badges
  • Rules and Prizes — if the organizer added them

No account needed. Anyone can view a tournament's Match Center without logging in — share the link with friends, family, or fans. A Login tab is right there if they decide to sign in.

05 Running a tournament Coordinator Admin

Creating one

Tournaments → New Tournament. Set the name, date/time, venue, season, description, and rules. Turn on Auction Mode if teams should be formed through a player auction instead of manual assignment — see Player auctions.

Building teams

On the tournament's Team Building tab: create teams, upload team logos, and drag players from the unassigned pool into a team. The Picker Wheel can randomly decide things like captain toss order. Export the roster to Excel at any time.

Rounds, groups, and fixtures

Once teams exist, the Fixtures tab unlocks the full structure:

  1. In the Tournament tab, build rounds (Group-Based or Direct Knockout), add groups, assign teams, and generate matches. Set advancement rules for which group positions move on.
  2. In the Matches tab, review and edit the schedule — teams, venue, date/time — in calendar, list, card, or timeline view.
  3. The Overview tab tracks round/group/match counts, completion progress, and top scorers.

Live match scoring

From a fixture, click Start Match and choose a venue. While play is live, record Goal, Assist, Yellow Card, Red Card, Substitution, and Injury events by team, player, and minute — the score updates as you go. Click Complete Match when it's over.

Prizes

Add prizes under a tournament's Prizes tab — team or player, position, category, amount, and an optional description or image.

06 Player auctions

Some tournaments form their teams through an auction instead of manual assignment. Open Auction in the sidebar for a list of auction tournaments, each with a live status: Not Started, Running, Paused, or Completed.

Registering

  • Already have an account? Click Register Me for This Auction — instant, using your existing profile.
  • New to the club? Fill in the public registration form — name, email, employee ID, phone, preferred position, availability, experience, and an optional photo. An admin reviews and approves it; once approved, an account is created for you with a temporary password to change on first login.

Watching live

Click Live Auction to follow along: the player currently up for bid, a countdown timer, bid history, and every team's remaining budget.

If you're a team owner

Place bids in preset increments while a player is up for auction, and check your squad and remaining budget at any point.

Admin auction management

Admins configure Settings (starting budget, bid increments, timer length, squad limits), manage the Player Pool and price categories, set up Team Budgets and owners, review registrations, and run the live auction — start, pause, next player, sell, mark unsold, or end.

07 Finance

Everyone can view financial records; only Admins and Super Admins can create or edit them.

  • Collections (+) — payments received from players, with monthly filters and a per-player chart
  • Bill Payment (-) — club expenses by cost type
  • Voucher Register — a combined transaction ledger
  • Accounts Reports — Accounts Report, Balances Summary, and Balances Sheet
  • Configuration Admin — the Voucher Types, AC Natures, and Chart of Account tables used across finance

08 Venues & club rules

Venues lists the grounds used for matches; admins can add or edit them. Club Rules holds the club's official rules, readable by everyone, editable by admins.

09 Account settings

From your avatar menu, top right, you can switch between dark and light mode (your choice is remembered on this device), open Settings to change your password or update your profile, check your Goalkeeping History, or log out.

11 Troubleshooting & FAQ

I can't see a button or menu I expected.
It's likely gated to a role above yours — check the table above. Ask an admin if you think you need access.
I forgot my password.
Ask an admin or coordinator to reset it from the Players list. You'll set a new one on your next sign-in.
A tournament isn't showing fixtures yet.
Fixtures only appear once teams have been built and matches generated by a coordinator or admin.
I don't have an account but want to join an auction.
Use the public registration form linked from the auction page — no login required to apply.
Can I share a tournament with someone who doesn't have an account?
Yes — the Match Center (fixtures, results, standings, stats, players, rules) is public. Just share the link.
